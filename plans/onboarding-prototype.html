Proposed flow

1Born→ 2Lived→ 3Learned→ 4Worked→ 5Partner→ 6Kids→ 7Grandkids→ Canvas, zoomed to your life

Rules it rests on

  • One topic, one screen, one orderEverything about a topic is asked in one place. The order is the same at every age.
  • Ages and years side by side"At 19" is easier to recall than "2005". Show both.
  • One editor for "one thing after another"Places, education and work share the strip and the list.
  • Gaps hold time, nothing moreA gap keeps the order honest and creates no entry.
  • A year is enoughStored with year precision. Month or day can be refined later on the canvas.
  • Always show the resultThe preview grows with every answer. No reveal screen to wait for.
